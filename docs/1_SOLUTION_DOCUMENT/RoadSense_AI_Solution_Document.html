1. Executive Summary & Problem Formulation

Modern commercial vehicle fleets generate up to 25 GB of time-series telemetry per hour across OBD-II diagnostic interfaces, CAN-bus controllers, GPS transceivers, battery management systems (BMS), and inertial measurement unit (IMU) accelerometers. For an enterprise operating 100,000+ vehicles, this produces a continuous ingestion firehose of ~100,000 events/second (~8.6 TB/day).

Core Industry Bottlenecks:

  • Ingestion Velocity & OEM Fragmentation: Disparate proprietary telematics signals across Tata, Mahindra, Ashok Leyland, BharatBenz, and Hyundai cause high cloud ingestion latency and packet loss.
  • Corridor Congestion & Road Degradation: Unmonitored road stress and traffic bottlenecks cause delivery delays, elevated fuel consumption, and vehicle wear across major Indian freight routes.
  • Delayed Public Safety Escalation: Traditional municipal road repair and emergency response depend on slow manual reports, creating hours of delay before hazards or accidents are cleared.

4. Polyglot Database Architecture & 3NF Schema

RoadSense AI leverages a 4-tier storage model to balance throughput, ACID compliance, and sub-millisecond retrieval:

  • PostgreSQL 15 3NF Core: Normalized relational storage for tenants, fleets, vehicles, drivers, trips, and audit trails.
  • TimescaleDB / Range Partitioning: Monthly partitioned tables on vehicle_telemetry(vehicle_id, event_timestamp) ensuring linear write scalability into billions of rows.
  • Redis 7.2 Cache: In-memory storage for active vehicle GPS coordinates, live speeds, and corridor stress scores.
  • pgvector Store: 1536-dimensional embeddings for incident retrieval and RAG search by the RoadSense AI agent.

5. Core Algorithmic Suite & Big-O Complexity

All core algorithms are encapsulated in the @roadsense/validation package with 100% test coverage:

  1. 17-Character VIN Validator (ISO 3779 / 3780): Rejects ambiguous characters (I, O, Q) and calculates MOD 11 check digits using official positional weights. Complexity: Time O(1), Space O(1).
  2. OBD-II DTC Diagnostic Fault Code Parser (SAE J2019 / ISO 15031-6): Evaluates powertrain (P), chassis (C), body (B), and network (U) codes with automatic severity ratings and corrective maintenance directives.
  3. EV Range & Dijkstra Optimal Charger Routing: Evaluates vehicle State-of-Charge (SoC), battery degradation factor, and Haversine distance to calculate recharge time and cost in INR. Complexity: Time O(N log N).
  4. Streaming Bloom Filter: Evaluates 500,000 keys with a 1% false positive rate using dual-hash bitwise manipulation, preventing duplicate processing during 3x network burst spikes. Complexity: Time O(k), Space O(m).

7. Government & Municipal Integration Roadmap

  • CAD Emergency Dispatch (Dial 112 / 1033): Automated generation of emergency packets to State Traffic Police Control Rooms when multiple IMU sensors detect collisions.
  • NHAI Highway Incident Protocol: Real-time webhooks delivering geo-fenced bottleneck data for automated Variable Message Sign (VMS) updates along National Highways (NH-44, Delhi-Mumbai Expressway).
  • Municipal Corporation Pothole Ticketing: Aggregated vertical accelerometer G-spikes trigger automated public works repair work orders with GPS coordinates and severity scores.
